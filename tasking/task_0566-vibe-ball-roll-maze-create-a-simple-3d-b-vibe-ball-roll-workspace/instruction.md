# The maze levels can't be completed

## What you see

Rolling the ball over gems doesn't feel right: the counter in the top bar lags behind what you've actually collected, so it never seems to reflect the gems you've swept up. Even after clearing every last gem on the board, rolling onto the exit ring does nothing at all — the level just keeps going and there's no way to finish it. On top of that, the gem panel jumps up out of line with the other readouts the moment you collect something, and the top of it gets clipped off by the edge of the screen so you can't read it properly.

Falling is just as unpredictable. The ball drops out of play while it's clearly sitting on solid wood next to a gap, yet it will happily hover with part of itself hanging over an actual hole without falling in. It's impossible to judge where it's safe to roll.

## What correct looks like

The gem readout should always match what you've picked up, and it should sit neatly in line with the other panels in the top bar, fully readable. Once the last gem is collected, touching the exit ring should complete the level. And the ball should only be lost when it genuinely rolls out over a gap — never while it's resting on solid floor.
