# The puzzle board stops behaving once screws get shuffled around

## What you see

Playing SCREW OUT normally is fine at first, but as soon as a screw has been carried over to another plank and then lifted and set down again, the board loses track of itself. A plank that clearly has nothing left fastened in it just sits there refusing to drop, while a different plank slides away even though you can plainly see a screw still fixed in it. Undoing a move that cleared several planks and then playing it again can leave the last plank on the board unsolvable — its screw sticks to the cursor with nowhere to put it, so there's no way to finish. And if you park a screw in the tray and clear the planks, the game throws up its cleared-board celebration while that screw is still sitting right there in the open.

On top of that, the bar across the top is a mess: the clock panel swallows almost the entire width and pushes the undo, restart and pause buttons off past the edge of the screen, so you can't see or tap them at all.

## What correct looks like

A plank should drop precisely when nothing is fastened in it, and stay firmly in place while a screw remains — however many times screws have been moved, undone or re-placed. The final plank should always be emptiable so every board can be completed, and the celebration should only arrive when every screw is truly gone, tray included. The top bar should sit comfortably within the screen with the level marker, the clock and the undo, restart and pause controls all visible and reachable.
