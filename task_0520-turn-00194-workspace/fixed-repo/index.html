HOW TO PLAY

  • 🔨 Click a screw to lift it out — it floats with your cursor. Click again to put it back.
  • ◉ While holding a screw, empty holes glow teal. Click one to screw it in — that hole’s plank is now pinned.
  • 💿 A plank holding zero screws falls off — and takes its holes with it. Don’t strand screws on a plank you can’t empty!
  • 🔒 Holes & screws buried under a plank are dim and locked until that plank drops.
  • ⏰ Drop every plank before the clock runs out. Made a bad move? ↶ UNDO takes it back; ↺ restarts the board.
  • ⌨ Keyboard: Tab/arrows pick, Enter lifts/places, Ctrl+Z undoes, Esc cancels/pauses.
